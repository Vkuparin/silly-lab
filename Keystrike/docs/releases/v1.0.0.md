# Keystrike v1.0.0 — first full release

Two twelve-mission campaigns defend Helsinki from the Scrap King's scavenger fleets. This is the first full release; v0.1.0 remains the historical playtest. The initial player feedback guided the improvements and the return campaign.

- Twenty-four mission scenes, individual level and commander themes, distinct commander damage cues, large entrances, genuine boss phases, visible ship damage and varied defeats. Cannon aiming, scrap popups, wave progress and the city approach boundary make actions and threats clearer.
- Standard, Relaxed and stronger Pro have consistent labeled presentation. Spectacular is the default; reduced motion still overrides moving effects. Standalone Practice is removed; untimed briefing rehearsal stays.
- Primary-only, two-button and opt-in extended mouse profiles. Middle click enters at L7 and Mouse 4 at L9, including Mixed missions without a new key group. The extended profile requires the displayed button check. Compatible fallback routes remain available.
- Defeat the campaign-one L12 Scrap King to unlock his twelve-mission return for the same route. Keyboard/Mixed commanders show whole localized words beneath their ships; every correct letter fires the cannon, with no typing popup, with shorter miniboss words and a seeded final sentence. Mouse stays pointer-only. New text characters are rehearsed; twelve sequel mission victories earn cannon appearances.
- Results lead with the finished attempt's Highscores board, refresh after explicit Save, and offer another eligible replay difficulty. Nicknames remain optional. Changed rules, device profiles, word languages and final sentence variants have separate comparisons; old boards remain available.
- Purchased cosmetic costs increase approximately 50%, rounded up. Existing balance, ownership and equipment are preserved. Every appearance remains cosmetic.
- Window presets through 5120×1440 on supported monitors and native fullscreen. Ultrawide scenery extends around a consistent logical arena; pointer geometry and simulation do not change with resolution.

## Downloads and upgrading

Windows x64, unsigned executable. Download the standalone EXE or extract the portable ZIP into a **writable folder**. The ZIP includes the same EXE, quick-start instructions and license notices. Microsoft **WebView2 Runtime is required and is not bundled**. No installer, account, backend or downloaded runtime assets.

Keep the existing `data/` beside the EXE when upgrading. Schema-1 saves migrate to schema 2 without removing purchases, currency, scores or lesson progress. Practice settings become Relaxed; Calm becomes reduced motion. Prior completion can mean a commander escaped: recorded defeat evidence unlocks the return campaign, otherwise an L12 rematch is needed. Unlocks never depend on choosing Save score.

All Keystrike-owned saves, backups **and WebView2 profile/cache** remain in executable-local `data/`. No marker and no AppData fallback. Read-only startup fails clearly; failed writes keep explicit pending rewards and cannot grant purchases. Move the entire folder to transfer progress.

## Validation and limits

See [verification evidence](../verification-v1.0.0.md) for the final build's automated, browser and native checks. Both-campaign simulations cover tracks/presets/locales/profiles, input ownership, phases, scoring, migration and reward transactions. Browser review uses actual input for endings, repeated word entry, extended logical buttons and ultrawide layout; native smoke exercises the packaged WebView2 app and portable data paths.

Timing and learning efficacy are not certified by automation. Supervised beginner/expert sessions, school-laptop performance, physical vendor Mouse 4 mappings, OS scaling across monitors and full real-layout Sticky/Filter Keys/IME coverage remain human/device validation. Choose the compatible mouse/Shift profile when hardware remaps inputs. Text encounters support committed character entry; paste/autofill shortcuts are blocked, and broad IME compatibility is not claimed.
